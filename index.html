About

I am an independent French–English interpreter based in Geneva. Cambridge-certified language teacher and approved examiner, I combine 20 years of language teaching experience with professional interpretation expertise for international organizations, NGOs, law firms and events.

Native bilingual (English mother, French father, raised in France) with complete command of the cultural and linguistic nuances of both languages. Every assignment is carefully prepared: terminology research, document familiarization, context study, and follow-up.

My goal: give you direct access to a professional, prepared and accountable interpreter.
