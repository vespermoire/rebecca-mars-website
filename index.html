About Rebecca

I am an independent French–English interpreter and translator based in the Geneva region. Cambridge-certified language teacher and approved examiner, I combine 20 years of language teaching experience with professional interpretation expertise for international organizations, NGOs, law firms and events.

Native bilingual (English mother, French father, raised in France) with complete command of the cultural and linguistic nuances of both languages. Every assignment is carefully prepared: terminology research, document familiarization, context study, and professional follow-up.

My commitment: direct access to a prepared, accountable professional interpreter.
