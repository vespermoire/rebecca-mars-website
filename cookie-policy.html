Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and enhance your experience.

2. Cookies We Use

Rebecca Mars website uses the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly:

  • Browser Local Storage: We use local storage (not traditional cookies) to remember your language preference (EN/FR) and currency choice (CHF/EUR) to personalize your experience

Non-Essential Cookies

Our website does not use analytics, marketing, or tracking cookies that require your consent.

3. How We Use Information from Local Storage

The information stored in your browser's local storage is used only to:

  • Maintain your selected language when you navigate the site
  • Display pricing in your preferred currency
  • Provide a personalized browsing experience

This data is stored only on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website includes links to third-party services (WhatsApp, social media) that may use their own cookies. Please review their privacy policies for information about their cookie practices.

5. Managing Cookies and Local Storage

You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear Browsing Data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Clear Browsing Data

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable local storage, your language and currency preferences will not be remembered, and you will see default settings (English and CHF) on each visit.
